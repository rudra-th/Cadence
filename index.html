Two tools, one rhythm

The plan writes the day down.
The clock runs it.

Most timers want you to remember what you were doing. Cadence keeps the whole day on one canvas and lets the timer follow along, so the two halves never drift apart.

The planner

A full day on one canvas. Pick an activity and drag across the grid; blocks merge as you go, split as you need them, and land in a list you can start from with a single tap.

  • 15-minute grid across the whole 24 hours
  • Your own activities, alongside the built-in ones
  • Tasks for the day, ticked off as you go
  • Copy a day, or clear it and start again

How it works

Three steps, then it is on its own

01

Paint the day

Choose an activity and drag it across the grid. Blocks join up when they touch and split when you paint over them.

02

Let the clock pick it up

Switch to the clock and the block that is running now is waiting for you. Start it by hand, or switch on auto-run and never press a thing.

03

Watch it add up

Every session lands in the log with the name of the block it came from, so the week tells you where the hours actually went.

Built to be local

Nothing leaves the device

Cadence is a folder of files and a browser. There is no server to call, no token to refresh, and no reason for your day to be sitting on somebody else’s computer.

Works with the network off

The service worker keeps every page and asset on the device, so a train, a plane, or a dead router changes nothing.

No account, ever

There is no sign-up, no email, no analytics. Your plans and your hours stay in this browser’s storage and nowhere else.

Exact, not approximate

Timing is derived from timestamps, never from tick counts, so the clock stays right when the tab is throttled or the screen sleeps.

Your data is yours

Export everything as JSON at any time, and erase it just as easily. No export maze, no “contact support”.

Give the day a shape

Paint tomorrow, then let the clock run it. Everything is stored on this device — nothing to sign up for.
