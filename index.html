( The idea )

Most planners hand you a list and call it a plan. Cadence gives you back the day itself — a finite, visible, honest shape. Place what matters, then stop negotiating with the clock and start living inside it.

How it works

Three moves.
No ceremony.

A plan you can read in a second, and a clock that only ever asks for the next block. Nothing to configure, nothing to maintain.

01

Paint the day.

A full 24-hour grid, one drag at a time. Name a block, give it a length, put it where it belongs. Import a timetable if you already have one.

02

Follow the clock.

Start the timer and the page becomes one block, one countdown, one decision. When it ends, the next block is already waiting.

03

Keep the record.

At the end the grid is no longer a plan. It's a record of what you actually did — ready to print, export, or keep for tomorrow.

Built for focus

Less interface.
More day.

Every part of Cadence exists to get you back to the work. There is no feed, no streak, no gamified guilt — just the day and the clock.

01 / One view

Your whole day.
One page.

Not a running list that hides the shape of your time. See all 24 hours at once, before you live them.

02 / One thing

One thing now.

The timer strips the day down to the block you're in and the time that's left.

03 / Offline

Offline by design.

A local service worker means it opens instantly with no network and no spinner.

04 / Portability

Yours to move.

Import a timetable, export your data, print the day. Nothing is locked in.

05 / Quiet

Quiet by default.

No analytics, no trackers, no notifications shouting for attention.
